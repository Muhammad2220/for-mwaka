To Mwaka,

I'm not always good with words when I say them out loud, so I built you something instead — a small piece of the sky, made of code, but meant entirely for you.

Every star up there is a moment. Every line connecting them is a choice I keep making, over and over, without even having to think about it — the choice to be here, with you.

You make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering. I don't know how you do that, but I hope you know how much it means.

This is just the beginning of the constellation. There's so much more sky left, and I want to fill every bit of it with you.

— yours, always
made with code, because that's the language I know best
